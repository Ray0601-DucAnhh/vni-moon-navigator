# Texture provenance

- Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_poles_4k.tif (NASA SVS “CGI Moon Kit”, svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720; visualisation by Ernie Wright, from the LROC WAC mosaic with LOLA data filling the poles).
- Processing: resized to 4096x2048 / 2048x1024 and JPEG-compressed by scripts/assets/prepare-texture.ts.
- NASA SVS describes the map as “optimized for aesthetics, not science”; the app labels surface shading as illustrative.
- NASA media usage guidelines apply (NASA imagery is generally not copyrighted; NASA does not endorse this project).

## Earth texture (earth_bluemarble_1k.jpg)
- Source: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/april/world.topo.bathy.200404.3x5400x2700.jpg - NASA Earth Observatory “Blue Marble Next Generation” with topography and bathymetry (R. Stöckli, NASA GSFC), April 2004 composite.
- Processing: resized to 1024x512 JPEG by scripts/assets/prepare-earth-texture.ts.
- Used only for the illustrative 3D Earth model (size, distance and rotation are not to scale / not modelled).
